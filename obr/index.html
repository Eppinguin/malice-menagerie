How to use

Connect, then press Send tokens in Malice’s top bar. Keep the app and room tabs open. You can close this panel while playing.

You can also connect from Malice’s Settings → Integrations → Owlbear Rodeo panel with your room link and this extension’s manifest URL.

Settings are saved in this browser.
